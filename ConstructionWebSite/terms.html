Acceptance of Terms

2

Scope of Services

DNA LANDMARKZ provides professional building, architectural, and design solutions including:

  • Turnkey Building Construction: Complete structural civil construction from site excavation and foundation to roofing, brickwork, plastering, plumbing, electrical conduit fitting, and final occupancy-ready finishing.
  • Architectural Design & 3D Elevations: Preparation of structural floor plans, elevation renders, working drawings, column layout schedules, and municipal sanction drawings.
  • Luxury Interior Design & Fit-outs: Modular kitchens, custom wardrobes, false ceilings, electrical lighting layouts, flooring installations, and wall panelling.
  • Full Home Renovation: Structural repairs, elevation remodeling, waterproofing, plumbing revamp, and floor additions.
3

Estimates, Quotations & Contracts

All initial estimates provided via website forms, telephone calls, or digital consultations are indicative approximations based on standard built-up area rates in Chennai. Formal quotations become binding only upon physical site inspection, soil verification, structural design sign-off, and mutual execution of a detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

Formal project quotes remain valid for 30 days from the date of issuance, subject to prevailing market prices for primary construction commodities (cement, steel, and sand).

4

Payment Milestones & Schedules

To ensure transparent, escrow-grade financial accountability, DNA LANDMARKZ utilizes a milestone-based stage payment schedule:

  • Stage 1 (Booking & Soil Investigation): 10% upon contract signing and mobilization.
  • Stage 2 (Foundation & Plinth Level): 15% upon completion of excavation, PCC, footings, and plinth beam casting.
  • Stage 3 (Ground Floor Slab Casting): 20% upon casting the structural RCC slab.
  • Stage 4 (Brickwork & First Floor Slab): 20% upon brick masonry and upper structural slabs.
  • Stage 5 (Plastering & MEP Conduit): 15% upon internal/external plastering, plumbing lines, and electrical conduits.
  • Stage 6 (Flooring, Painting & Fixtures): 15% upon tile laying, prime painting, and sanitaryware fixing.
  • Stage 7 (Final Inspection & Key Handover): 5% upon joint client inspection and snag rectification.
Payment Transparency

All payments must be remitted via bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) or crossed account payee cheques made in favor of DNA LANDMARKZ. Official GST tax receipts are issued for every transaction.

5

Client Responsibilities & Site Access

To ensure on-time delivery and smooth site operations, the Client is responsible for:

  • Providing unimpeded physical access to the project plot for heavy machinery, concrete transit mixers, and site personnel.
  • Securing municipal plan sanctions (CMDA, DTCP, or local Panchayat approvals) unless DNA LANDMARKZ has been specifically retained for sanction liaison services.
  • Ensuring uninterrupted electricity and water connections suitable for concrete curing and construction works.
  • Reviewing and approving material samples (tiles, paint shades, bathroom fixtures) within 7 calendar days of presentation to avoid site downtime.
6

Execution Timelines & Change Orders

Project completion timelines are estimated based on normal working conditions. Unavoidable delays resulting from severe weather (monsoons, cyclones), government curfew/strikes, unprecedented material shortages, or delayed milestone payments shall extend the completion timeline reasonably under Force Majeure doctrines.

Any architectural modifications or material specification changes requested after structural work has commenced require a written Change Order detailing cost adjustments and schedule modifications.

7

Structural Warranty & Quality Assurance

DNA LANDMARKZ stands behind every square foot we construct with uncompromising quality standards:

  • 10-Year Structural Guarantee: Full structural warranty covering core RCC columns, beams, foundations, and slab stability against structural defects.
  • 12-Month Defect Liability Period: Free maintenance and repair of electrical wiring, plumbing connections, tile alignment, and hardware fittings following handover.
  • Branded Materials Only: We deploy exclusively ISI-certified 53-grade cement (Ultratech / Ramco), Fe-550 TMT steel (Tata Tiscon / JSW), premium wires (Finolex / Havells), and CPVC plumbing lines (Ashirvad / Astral).
